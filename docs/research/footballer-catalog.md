# Footballer catalog

Researched 2026-11-13. This is a fixed catalog of **500 distinct association football players**, suitable for readable local branch/workspace names. It is an editorial selection across historical and modern men's and women's football, not an objective ranking of the 500 greatest players. Inclusion does not assert current playing status. Each person appears once, using a lowercase ASCII slug.

## Selection and provenance

The catalog combines three first-party editorial rosters: each publisher is the primary source for its own selection, rather than an authority establishing an objective sporting rank.

- Entries 1–100 retain every player in [The Football History Boys' Top 100](https://www.thefootballhistoryboys.com/p/the-football-history-boys-top-100.html), in its displayed 100-to-1 order, with the identity and spelling corrections below. The source's order is retained only for traceability.
- Entries 101–112 retain the requested additions, in order: Neymar, Kylian Mbappé, Erling Haaland, Robert Lewandowski, Luka Modrić, Mohamed Salah, Marta, Mia Hamm, Birgit Prinz, Abby Wambach, Alexia Putellas and Aitana Bonmatí. Haaland's identity is independently supported by [Manchester City's player announcement](https://www.mancity.com/news/mens/erling-haaland-10-year-manchester-city-contract-63872694); the other additions also occur in the editorial rosters below.
- Entries 113–206 add the remaining 94 distinct players from [FourFourTwo's Top 100 women's footballers of all time](https://www.fourfourtwo.com/news/ranked-top-100-womens-footballers-of-all-time), in displayed order. Its editors describe selection using ability, statistics, success and pioneering influence. The page has a repeated rank 15 and no rank 25; it nevertheless supplies 100 distinct player names. All 100 identities are retained, including the six already added in entries 107–112.
- Entries 207–500 add 294 selected players from World Soccer's own [2018 alphabetical 500-player roster](https://www.worldsoccer.com/world-soccer-latest/world-soccer-500-2018-edition-368791). Its criterion is editorial importance in 2018, not an all-time ability ranking. Selection here favors recognizable names across countries and roles while avoiding duplicates with the retained groups. World Soccer's other pages support the following surname ranges: [Ben Yedder–Courtois](https://www.worldsoccer.com/world-soccer-latest/world-soccer-500-2018-edition-368791/2), [Coutinho–Fathy](https://www.worldsoccer.com/world-soccer-latest/world-soccer-500-2018-edition-368791/3), [Fekir–Hazard](https://www.worldsoccer.com/world-soccer-latest/world-soccer-500-2018-edition-368791/4), [Hector–Labyad](https://www.worldsoccer.com/world-soccer-latest/world-soccer-500-2018-edition-368791/5), [Lang–Mkhitaryan](https://www.worldsoccer.com/world-soccer-latest/world-soccer-500-2018-edition-368791/6), [Modrić–Perišić](https://www.worldsoccer.com/world-soccer-latest/world-soccer-500-2018-edition-368791/7), [Petersen–Sánchez](https://www.worldsoccer.com/world-soccer-latest/world-soccer-500-2018-edition-368791/8), [Sánchez–Tielemans](https://www.worldsoccer.com/world-soccer-latest/world-soccer-500-2018-edition-368791/9), and [Toivonen–Żurkowski](https://www.worldsoccer.com/world-soccer-latest/world-soccer-500-2018-edition-368791/10). Club and nationality context on those pages identifies the intended players; historical club associations are not current-club claims.

## Normalization and disambiguation

Use familiar playing names, lowercase them, transliterate accents to readable ASCII, and separate words with single hyphens. Remove apostrophes and honorifics. For example, Pelé becomes `pele`, Müller becomes `muller`, Ødegaard becomes `odegaard`, and Kelley O'Hara becomes `kelley-ohara`. This is a curated name catalog, not a general identity resolver: short playing names such as `marta`, `cafu`, `pele`, `arthur`, `marcelo` and `pepe` deliberately identify the footballer in the cited roster. Do not add a second entry for an alias or a diacritic variant.
